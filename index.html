How to Use This App

Step 1 — Export data from Emporia

  1. 1.Open the Emporia Web App menu:
    web.emporiaenergy.com/menu
  2. 2.Select "Export Raw Data to CSV" and choose the Arterra Emporia device.
  3. 3.Set the Start and End Date to match the paper billing period exactly.
  4. 4.Click "Export Data" — you will receive an email with a download link to a ZIP file.

Step 2 — Upload the ZIP

Drop the downloaded .zip file into the upload area. Do not unzip it — the app extracts the hourly CSV automatically.

Step 3 — Enter the paper bill total

Type the exact Total Amount Due from the PG&E / CleanPowerSF paper bill. The app uses this to scale every parking space's bill proportionally so the grand total matches the paper bill to the penny — absorbing all fixed fees, taxes, and surcharges automatically.

Step 4 — Select the tariff

Choose the active billing schedule:

  • Schedule B-6 — General Commercial TOU (current default)
  • Schedule BEV1 — Dedicated Business EV (if the HOA switches in future)

Understanding the Pro-Rata Multiplier

The multiplier badge (e.g. x1.29144) bridges the gap between the raw energy cost and the actual paper bill. It absorbs all fixed charges, customer fees, SF utility taxes, and PG&E surcharges.

Multiplier = Paper Bill ÷ Sum of Raw TOU Costs
Final Bill = Raw TOU Cost × Multiplier

Updating Tariff Rates

Rates typically change on July 1 each year. Edit rates.json in the GitHub repository — no other file needs to change. Sources:

  • PG&E Schedule B-6 tariff sheet
  • CleanPowerSF rates page

Use the Advanced panel on the main page to test new rates in-session before committing them to the file.

Data Privacy

The Emporia ZIP file is parsed entirely in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server or stored anywhere outside this browser tab.
